# Askwell — Questions & Answers for Confluence

Askwell gives your Confluence site a place to ask questions and keep the answers: a searchable Q&A page, a **Q&A** button on every page, and a macro that shows a topic's questions wherever you need them.

- **Ask from any page.** The Q&A button at the top of each page lists the questions asked about it and lets readers ask in place. Questions about a restricted page are only shown to people who can see that page.
- **Find answers before asking.** Search covers titles and full text, and similar questions appear while you type a new one.
- **Votes and accepted answers.** The best answer rises to the top; the asker or a moderator marks the one that solved it.
- **Topics.** Tag questions, filter by topic, and optionally limit topics to a fixed list.
- **FAQ pages that maintain themselves.** The macro's FAQ view shows solved questions with their accepted answers for a topic, space or page.
- **CSV import.** Bring existing questions and answers in from a spreadsheet.
- **Control who asks and who answers.** Allow everyone, or only members of chosen groups. Moderators can edit, delete and accept anywhere.
- **Works with Rovo.** The Askwell Q&A agent answers from your team's questions in Rovo chat.
- **Data stays in Atlassian.** Runs on Atlassian Forge with no outside servers.
