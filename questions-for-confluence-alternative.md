# A Questions for Confluence alternative for Confluence Cloud

Teams that relied on Q&A in Confluence Server or Data Center often look for the same thing in Confluence Cloud: a place to ask, answer and find questions next to their documentation. This page compares Askwell with the options on the Atlassian Marketplace, using what each listing states publicly (October 2026).

## What teams ask for

Reviews of Q&A apps for Confluence Cloud keep naming the same needs:

- Questions should be **visible where people read**, not only on a separate page.
- **Search** should cover the question and answer text, not just titles.
- Admins want to **control who asks and who answers**, and to keep topics tidy.
- Questions about restricted pages must **stay restricted**.

## How Askwell covers them

| Need | Askwell |
|---|---|
| Visible where people read | **Q&A** button on every page and blog post, plus a macro for any page |
| Full-text search | Titles and text, topic filters, similar questions while typing |
| Who asks / who answers | Everyone, or members of chosen groups; moderators |
| Tidy topics | Free topics or a fixed list set by admins |
| Restricted pages | Questions linked to a page are shown only to people who can see it |
| Where data lives | Runs on Atlassian (Forge); no outside servers |
| Price | Free for up to 10 users; per-user pricing above that on the Marketplace |

## Moving from Data Center

Askwell runs on Confluence Cloud only. Admins can bring existing questions and answers in with a CSV import (title, details, topics, answer; up to 100 rows per run), so the answers your teams rely on are searchable from day one. Imported answers are marked accepted and appear in FAQ macros.

## Try it

Install Askwell from the Atlassian Marketplace (search "Askwell"). It is free for sites with up to 10 users. Read the [user guide](guide.html) or [contact support](support.html).
