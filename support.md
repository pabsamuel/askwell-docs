# Support

**App:** Askwell — Questions & Answers for Confluence · **Publisher:** Atesen Software
**Email:** sametatesen2@gmail.com · **First response target:** 2 business days (Monday–Friday, Istanbul time, UTC+3)

## Getting started

Open **Apps → Q&A** in the Confluence top bar, or select **Q&A** at the top of any page. Admins configure the app in **Confluence administration → Askwell Q&A**. Full steps: [user guide](guide.html).

## Before you write

- Your site URL (`yoursite.atlassian.net`)
- Where you were (Q&A page, page button, macro or settings) and what you did
- The message shown in the app

## Severity and response

| Severity | Example | Target |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | A question about a restricted page is visible to someone who cannot see the page | Same-day acknowledgement, fix within 2 business days |
| S2 | Asking, answering, voting or search doesn't work | 3 business days |
| S3 | Question or feature request | Answered in the next weekly pass |

## Known limits

- Askwell does not send email notifications yet. Use **Mine** on the Q&A page to follow your questions.
- Search matches words in titles and text; it does not rank by meaning.
- Lists show up to 50 questions at a time; use **Show more** for the rest.
